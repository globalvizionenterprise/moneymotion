GV DAW

Our Own All-In-One Workstation — Built In Public.

VIZION AUDIO LABS — the plugin company of GLOBAL VIZION ENTERPRISE, makers of CHOPS

GV DAW (working title) is in active development. Stage 1 — the core scaffold — is built and compiled. There is no download and no release date yet: this page is the build log, and every stage gets proven before the next one starts.

Next GatesHeadless launch proof (the app running on a real audio device), then the timeline surface and working mixer faders. Plugin hosting is Stage 2 — designed, not built yet.

The Vision

One ProgramThe feature classes of the big workstations — pro tracking & mixing depth, pattern + piano-roll beat workflow, clip-launching live energy — as ONE program, not six subscriptions.

Tracks, Buses, MixerUnlimited audio & MIDI tracks, buses and sends, a full mixer, timeline editing with fades — the core a real DAW is judged on.

CHOPS InsideGV DAW is being built so CHOPS, our sample chopper, runs inside it from day one — the lab's own tools, native in the lab's own DAW.

Plugin Hosting — Stage 2On the roadmap: hosting VST3 / AU / CLAP plugins, the open standards every maker's plugins already speak.

Skins & ThemesA full theme system from the start: recolor the whole face, build and share your own skins. GV Dark Pro, GV Colorful and GV High Contrast are already in the Stage 1 code.

Every OS, Old To NewWindows, Mac and Linux from one codebase, with a low compatibility floor — if a machine can run a DAW, GV DAW aims to run on it.

Same family as CHOPS and Collab Bridge — all original code under VIZION AUDIO LABS. Functionality parity with the big DAWs is the goal; their code, graphics and names are never copied.

Build-In-Public Updates

Nov 18, 2026 — Stage 1 built and compiledThe first stage of GV DAW compiled clean: standalone JUCE app shell with transport (play / stop / record / click / tempo), audio + MIDI track list, the mixer model (channel strips, master + aux buses, sends), save/load of .gvdaw sessions, and the theme engine with three factory themes. Next: the headless launch proof, then the timeline and mixer surfaces.
