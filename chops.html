Load any sample and CHOPS auto-chops it into 4-bar and 8-bar loops — easier than an MPC, built for speed. Play the chops on the pads or the built-in keyboard, shape them with EQ and the HALL XL rack, split stems, and export every chop as its own WAV.

What It Does

Auto-ChopDrop in a sample — CHOPS slices it into 4-bar and 8-bar loops automatically, with a CUTOFF knob that ends each chop dead on point. No overlap.

16 RGB PadsFlashy Maschine-style pads with per-chop editing — voice mode, octave and speed per chop. Trigger from your MIDI controller (pads start at C3).

Built-In KeyboardA full keyboard strip docked under the pads — play chops pitched across the keys, with per-sound-set LED colours and a glide wheel.

10 SkinsGV Studio, Neon, Gold, Vintage, White and more — plus build your own skin with your own background picture and logo.

7-Band EQPro-class EQ with a drawn curve and real per-band LED meters that move with each band's actual level.

HALL XL ReverbA big hall reverb rack with freeze past 95% — plus the FX rack for glue and polish.

Stem SplitterSplit a loaded wave into drums, bass, vocals and other — right inside the plugin.

AI Co-ProducerGenerate 808s, kicks, snares, hats, bass, plucks and pads — plus a choir builder that sings the words you type.

ConsolidateExport every chop as its own WAV file, sample-for-sample, ready for any DAW or sampler.

  • Windows: run CHOPS-Setup.exe, then in FL Studio go to Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
  • Mac: open the .pkg — first time, right-click it and choose Open (the installer is unsigned).
  • Linux: unzip CHOPS-Linux-v119.zip and run bash install.sh — the VST3 lands in ~/.vst3, then rescan in REAPER, Ardour, Bitwig or your Linux DAW.
  • Formats: VST3, AU and Standalone — FL Studio, Ableton, Logic and all major DAWs.
